Need help with Alchemy TD? Write to us and we will get back to you as soon as possible, usually within a few days.

## Contact

Email: **alchemytd@gmail.com**

## Common questions

- **Progress and iCloud sync:** your progress is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is also kept in your private iCloud database. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud is enabled for the game in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
- **Restore purchases:** open the game's settings and choose *Restore Purchases* while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy them.
- **Analytics:** you can turn analytics on or off at any time in Settings → Privacy.

## Reporting a problem

To help us solve it faster, please include:

- Your device model and iOS (or Android) version.
- The app version (shown in the app's settings or on its App Store page).
- What you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Please do not send passwords or payment details.

## Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple (or Google). To request a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/); we cannot issue refunds directly.
