Need help with Little GP? Write to us and we will get back to you as soon as possible, usually within a few days.

## Contact

Email: **littlegpthegame@gmail.com**

## Common questions

- **Premium unlock:** it is a one-time purchase. Use *Restore* in the game's store screen while signed in with the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android) used to buy it.
- **Leaderboards (iOS):** make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings → Game Center.
- **Progress:** cars, tracks, records, ghost laps and your custom circuits are stored on your device only.

## Reporting a problem

To help us solve it faster, please include:

- Your device model and iOS (or Android) version.
- The app version (shown in the app's settings or on its App Store page).
- What you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Please do not send passwords or payment details.

## Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple (or Google). To request a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/); we cannot issue refunds directly.
