Need help with GuessWhat: Party Charades? Write to us and we will get back to you as soon as possible, usually within a few days.

## Contact

Email: **guesswhatpartycharades@gmail.com**

## Common questions

- **Restore purchases:** go to Options and choose *Restore Purchases* while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy your decks or full access.
- **Ads:** the free version shows ads; purchasing full access removes them. EU/UK users can review their ad consent in Options → Privacy settings.
- **Tracking permission:** you can change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## Reporting a problem

To help us solve it faster, please include:

- Your device model and iOS (or Android) version.
- The app version (shown in the app's settings or on its App Store page).
- What you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Please do not send passwords or payment details.

## Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple (or Google). To request a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/); we cannot issue refunds directly.
