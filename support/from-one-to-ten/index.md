Need help with From 1 to 10: Trivia Game? Write to us and we will get back to you as soon as possible, usually within a few days.

## Contact

Email: **fromonetotentheapp@gmail.com**

## Common questions

- **Offline play:** the game is designed to be played offline. Scores and player names stay on your device.
- **Restore purchases:** use the restore option in the app while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy them.
- **Lost data:** progress is stored locally, so uninstalling the app removes it.

## Reporting a problem

To help us solve it faster, please include:

- Your device model and iOS (or Android) version.
- The app version (shown in the app's settings or on its App Store page).
- What you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Please do not send passwords or payment details.

## Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple (or Google). To request a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/); we cannot issue refunds directly.
