Need help with Jam Mate: AI Backing Tracks? Write to us and we will get back to you as soon as possible, usually within a few days.

## Contact

Email: **jammatetheapp@gmail.com**

## Common questions

- **Premium and subscriptions:** use *Restore Purchases* in the app while signed in with the Apple Account used to subscribe. To cancel or manage a subscription, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
- **Saved jams:** jams and favorites are stored locally on your device and are lost if you uninstall the app.
- **No account needed:** Jam Mate does not require you to create an account.

## Reporting a problem

To help us solve it faster, please include:

- Your device model and iOS (or Android) version.
- The app version (shown in the app's settings or on its App Store page).
- What you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Please do not send passwords or payment details.

## Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple (or Google). To request a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/); we cannot issue refunds directly.
