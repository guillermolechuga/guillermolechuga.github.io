*Last updated: November 11, 2026*

This policy explains what information Little GP ("the game", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: you don't need an account, we never ask for your name or email inside the game, there are no ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## 1. Who is responsible

The game is developed and published by Guillermo Lechuga Rivas (Spain), who is the data controller.

Contact for any privacy question or request: **littlegpthegame@gmail.com**

## 2. Information the game handles

### Your game progress

Your progress (unlocked cars, tracks, cups and extras, medals, records, ghost laps, the circuits you build in the track editor and your settings) is stored only on your device. We cannot see or access it.

### Usage analytics

Little GP uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it. The events we send are limited to gameplay and app information, for example:

- A race started or finished: mode, track, car class, difficulty, number of laps and rivals, finishing position, race and best-lap times, and the medal earned.
- Screens opened and game states (menu, race, pause, results).
- Settings changed in the game (for example, sound or controls).
- The result of store operations (purchase, restore, check) and whether the premium unlock is active.
- Basic technical data collected by Firebase: device model, operating system and app version, language, and approximate country/region.

Our analytics events never include your name, email, Game Center identity, save data or the circuits you create. We don't set a user ID, we don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID), and analytics data is never used for advertising. Firebase is configured with its advertising signals and ad personalization turned off.

### Crash reports

Little GP uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports and fix bugs. A report may include the device model and OS version, the app version, the date and time of the crash, a technical stack trace, a technical installation identifier, and the state of the game when it crashed (for example, the screen and the race settings).

### Purchases

The premium unlock is a one-time purchase processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate the purchase and restore it on your devices; it receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase records (product, date, price, country). It does not receive your name or email.

### Game Center leaderboards (iOS)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best times may be submitted to the game's leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under your Apple Account: your Game Center nickname and scores are visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. We don't receive your Apple Account details. You can sign out of Game Center or change its visibility in iOS Settings → Game Center.

### Support requests

If you email us, we'll process your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message, only to answer you and solve the issue.

### What we don't collect

No account, name, email (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location or health data. The game shows no ads, sends no push notifications, has no chat and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 3. Why we use it (legal bases)

- **Performance of a contract:** delivering and restoring your purchases, and providing the game's features, including leaderboards.
- **Legitimate interest:** limited, non-advertising analytics and crash reports to keep the game working and improve it. We've kept this to the minimum needed and you can object at any time (see section 6).
- **Your request:** handling the emails you send us.
- **Legal obligations:** keeping purchase records where the law requires it.

## 4. Service providers

We share data only with the providers needed to run the game, each bound by its own privacy terms:

- Apple (App Store, payments, Game Center) — [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Google (Google Play, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics) — [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- RevenueCat (purchase validation) — [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

Some of these providers process data outside the European Union (for example, in the United States). Those transfers are covered by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Game progress:** on your device until you delete the game.
- **Analytics:** kept for a limited time (up to 14 months) and then deleted automatically.
- **Crash reports:** kept up to 90 days.
- **Purchase records:** as long as needed to restore purchases and meet legal obligations.
- **Support emails:** as long as needed to resolve your request.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU GDPR or California's CCPA), you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw any consent you've given. Because we don't collect your name or email in the game, we may need some detail from you (for example, the approximate date of a purchase or the device you play on) to find your data.

To exercise any right, write to **littlegpthegame@gmail.com**. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're in the EU, you can also file a complaint with your data protection authority (in Spain, the [Agencia Española de Protección de Datos](https://www.aepd.es/)).

**Deleting your data:** deleting the game removes all your progress from the device. For analytics, crash or purchase data, email us and we'll handle it with the relevant provider. Some purchase records that Apple, Google or RevenueCat must keep for legal or fraud-prevention reasons may not be deletable right away.

## 7. Children

Little GP is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The game does not ask anyone for personal details. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

We and our providers use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against loss, unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the game or on its store page.
