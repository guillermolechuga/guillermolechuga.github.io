title: Privacy Policy – From 1 to 10
---

*Last updated: May 10, 2026*

At From 1 to 10, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we handle your information. The short version: **we do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.**

## 1. Information collection and use

- **Personal data:** the app does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share your name to function. Any player names you enter in the game are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
- **In-app purchases:** all transactions are processed by Apple via the App Store. We do not have access to your credit card information or billing details.
- **Offline gameplay:** From 1 to 10 is designed to be played offline. Your gameplay history and scores stay on your device.

## 2. Analytics and crash reporting

To improve the app's performance, we may use third-party services (such as Apple's built-in App Analytics) that collect anonymous, non-identifying technical data. This includes:

- Device type and OS version.
- Crash logs (to help us fix bugs).
- Aggregated usage data (for example, how many people completed a game).

This data is used solely to provide a better experience and cannot be used to identify you.

## 3. Third-party services

The app may contain links to our official social media or support email. Once you leave the app, the privacy policies of those specific platforms apply.

## 4. Children's privacy

From 1 to 10 is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, we are fully compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

## 5. Your rights

Since we do not store your data on our servers, we do not have anything to delete or export. If you wish to remove all game data, simply uninstall the app from your device.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

## 7. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: **fromonetotentheapp@gmail.com**
