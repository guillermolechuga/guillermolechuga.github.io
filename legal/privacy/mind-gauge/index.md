*Last updated: June 8, 2026*

MindGauge respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app processes, how it is used, and your available choices.

## Data controller

- **Data controller:** Guillermo Lechuga
- **Contact:** mindgaugetheapp@gmail.com
- **Country:** Spain

## Information we process

MindGauge does not require an account or sign-in. It does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or health information.

The app may process:

- **Usage data:** visited screens, game modes, number of teams, selected packs, rounds, scores, and purchase or restoration results.
- **Technical data:** device model, operating system and app versions, technical identifiers, and diagnostic information.
- **Crash data:** technical logs associated with errors or unexpected crashes.
- **Purchase information:** purchased products, purchase status, and the receipts or identifiers required to verify them.

We do not receive or store your complete payment card details. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store.

## How we use information

We use this information to:

- Provide and maintain MindGauge.
- Store your settings and preferences locally.
- Process, verify, and restore purchases.
- Understand how the app is used through aggregated statistics.
- Diagnose errors and improve stability and gameplay.
- Respond to support requests.

Depending on the circumstances, processing is based on providing the requested service, compliance with legal obligations, our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app, or your consent where required.

## Information stored on your device

Settings, preferences, and certain information required by the app are stored locally on your device.

You can remove this local information by uninstalling MindGauge. Purchases remain associated with the Apple Account used to make them and may be restored where applicable.

## Third-party services

MindGauge uses the following service providers:

- Google Firebase Analytics, for usage statistics.
- Google Firebase Crashlytics, for error detection and diagnostics.
- RevenueCat, for managing and verifying in-app purchases.
- Apple, for distributing the app and processing payments.

These providers may process information outside the European Economic Area under their own privacy terms and data-protection mechanisms:

- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Data retention

Local information remains on your device until it is deleted or the app is uninstalled.

Third-party providers retain information for the periods necessary to provide their services, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and maintain system security.

Support messages may be retained for as long as necessary to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

## Children's privacy

MindGauge is a social game intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us to request its deletion.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request:

- Access to your personal information.
- Correction or deletion.
- Restriction of or objection to processing.
- Data portability, where applicable.
- Withdrawal of consent.

To exercise these rights, contact mindgaugetheapp@gmail.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority, such as the [Spanish Data Protection Agency](https://www.aepd.es/).

Some technical information is associated with installation identifiers rather than a personal account. We may need additional information to identify a specific installation or purchase.

## Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to MindGauge, its service providers, or applicable laws. The current version will remain available on this page with its latest revision date.
